# Titles and thumbnail text

Write 3 different YouTube title options for this video, plus 3 short thumbnail text ideas.

## Title rules
- Maximum 70 characters each.
- Say what the viewer will see or learn. Put the most important words first.
- The 3 options should differ in angle (for example: outcome, workflow, lesson learned).
- Follow the style guide. No clickbait, no hype, no invented claims.

## Thumbnail text rules
- 2 to 5 words each, readable at a glance.
- Must be supported by the video content.

## JSON keys
```json
{
  "titles": ["option 1", "option 2", "option 3"],
  "thumbnail_texts": ["idea 1", "idea 2", "idea 3"]
}
```
